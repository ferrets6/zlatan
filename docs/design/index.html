The mark

An arc you leave, a crossing, a bar you land on

The top arm is an arc, the orbit you are leaving. The bottom is a flat bar, the ground you land on. Between them, the crossing. Same compass as Nuno: 24 grid, two units of stroke, round caps, no fill.

The arc carries Google’s four colours and the colour stops at the crossing. Under 32 px the bands turn to mud, so small sizes use the monochrome file.

The palette

Nuno’s six values, plus one

RoleLightDarkFromContrast
Foreground #1c1c1a #e6e6e3 Nuno 16.5 : 1 / 14.5 : 1 on the background
Accent #3f7d4f #6fae7d Nuno 4.8 : 1 / 6.9 : 1 — also the button ground
Background #fbfbfa #15161a Nuno —
Attention #9c5841 #d1886e Added 5.2 : 1 / 6.4 : 1

Attention is the only addition: the accent’s own OKLCH lightness and chroma at hue 40, so it carries the same weight as the green. One meaning only: this needs you, or this stopped. Waiting gets no colour. Everything else is the foreground mixed into the background at a stated percentage.
