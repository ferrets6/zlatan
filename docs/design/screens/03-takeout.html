Ask Google for a copy of your photos

Five short steps, on Google’s own pages. You are on step three. Nothing here is urgent: this page remembers where you got to, so you can stop and come back tomorrow.

  1. 1 Opened Google Takeout Change this
  2. 2 Chose Google Photos, and nothing else Change this
  3. 3

    Tell Google where to put it

    At the bottom of the Takeout page, press Next step. Google then asks four things. Set them exactly like this:

    Where it goes

    Add to Drive

    How often

    Export once

    File type

    .zip

    File size

    50 GB

    Add to Drive is the one that matters: it puts the copy in your own Google Drive, where we can pick it up without you downloading anything. The large file size just means fewer pieces to keep track of.

Your Google storage might be too small

Add to Drive needs room in your Google account for the whole copy. If Google tells you it will not fit, do not delete anything to make space — come this way instead.
