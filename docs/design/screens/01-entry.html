Two moves, and they have nothing to do with each other: your files go to Nextcloud, your photos go to Immich. Do one, do both at the same time, or stop either one whenever you like.

Nothing is ever deleted from Google. Everything here is a copy.

There is one step only you can do.

In March 2025 Google closed the door that let apps read photo libraries — not just this one, every app. So the copy has to start with you asking Google for it. After that, you are done.

Why can’t zlatan do this part?

Until March 2025 a program could ask Google for permission to read your photos, and you could grant it on a single screen. Google removed that permission for everyone.

What is left is Google Takeout: a page where you ask Google for a copy of your own library. There is no way for a program to press that button for you — Google does not offer one.

So we do not pretend. You press it, we take it from there, and we will never show you a progress bar for something that is not moving.
