One step only you can do.

In March 2025 Google stopped letting apps read photo libraries. Any app. So you ask Google for the copy yourself, once.

{{ if .CanTakeout }}

The export lands in your own Google Drive, in a folder called {{ .TakeoutFolder }}. Nothing to share: we already have read-only access, and we pick it up from there.

{{ end }}
Why not?

Until March 2025 an app could ask permission to read your photos and you granted it on one screen. Google removed that permission.

What is left is Google Takeout, where you ask for a copy of your own library. No program can press that button for you.

So we do not pretend otherwise. You press it, we do the rest.

How long this takes. A few hours for most libraries, up to a day for a very large one. It often arrives in several parts and we wait for all of them, so nothing lands in Immich half-finished.

What happens when it arrives

  1. Copy it from your Drive to the home server
  2. Unpack it and read Google’s notes on each photo
  3. Restore the dates, places and albums
  4. Fill up Immich with the photos and their albums
